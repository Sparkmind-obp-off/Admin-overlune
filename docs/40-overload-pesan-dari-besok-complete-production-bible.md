# OVERLOAD — PESAN DARI BESOK
## Production Bible, Complete Screenplays, Final Storyboards & Genspark Prompt Pack

**Creative lock:** Indonesia · live-action cinematic · 8 episodes  
**Format:** vertical 9:16 · 1080×1920 · 24/25 fps consistently · 55–70 seconds/episode  
**Genre:** romantic mystery / psychological thriller / betrayal  
**Status:** written production package complete; generation, editing and publishing have not yet been performed.

## 1. Series premise
Nadia receives an anonymous message warning her not to go home with her boyfriend, Arka. The warning proves accurate. More messages arrive with evidence from events that have not happened yet. As Nadia investigates, she discovers Arka's betrayal, a woman named Rani who also receives messages, and recordings that appear to come from Nadia herself—seven days in the future.

**Series promise:** each episode answers one immediate question, reveals one new clue and ends on a specific cliffhanger. The concept is original and uses broad genre conventions only; do not copy scenes, dialogue, characters or plots from existing dramas.

## 2. Creative and visual direction
- Grounded contemporary Indonesia; believable apartment, café and residential parking locations.
- Restrained acting, intimate close-ups, controlled push-ins, natural skin texture and practical lighting.
- Palette: charcoal, deep blue, muted purple, warm amber practicals.
- Phone screens are kept blank/abstract during AI generation. Add all legible UI, messages, timestamps, maps and subtitles in post.
- No supernatural visual effects in Season 1. Preserve ambiguity around the sender's mechanism.
- Sound: phone vibration, room tone, footsteps, distant traffic, restrained low pulse; silence before cliffhangers.
- No watermarks, logos, random text, face morphing, extra fingers, duplicated props or wardrobe drift.

## 3. Character bible

### Nadia Putri — 25
Indonesian woman, shoulder-length dark hair, natural makeup, charcoal outer layer over muted neutral top, dark trousers, small silver ring. Smart, reserved, observant. Fear appears first in her eyes and breath; avoid melodrama. Arc: doubt → evidence gathering → confrontation → agency. Keep face, hairstyle, ring and wardrobe stable across shots.

### Arka Pratama — 27
Indonesian man, neat short dark hair, clean-shaven, dark casual jacket and plain shirt. Charming, persuasive, evasive under pressure. Betrayal should feel human and grounded, not cartoonishly villainous. Keep appearance and wardrobe stable.

### Rani — 26
Indonesian woman, long dark hair tied low, muted olive/earth-tone outfit. Guarded and exhausted, not a stereotypical antagonist. She is another person manipulated by the same strange messages.

### The Sender / Nadia-2
Do not create a new unrelated face. Use Nadia's reference identity only as a hand, shoulder, reflection, silhouette or out-of-focus profile until Episode 6. Episode 5 reveals the sender label “NADIA”; Episode 6 gives visual evidence of a recording made seven days earlier. The exact mechanism remains open for a later season.

### Props and locations
- Nadia phone: same dark smartphone and case throughout.
- Arka phone: visibly different device/case.
- Silver ring: recurring visual clue.
- Apartment: same table, sofa, door and warm lamp geography.
- Café: corner table, pendant lights, window reflections, no readable signage.
- Corridor/parking: cool overhead lighting, consistent door and camera direction.

# 5. Complete screenplays

## EP01 — PESAN PERTAMA (60 sec)
**INT. APARTMENT — NIGHT**

**0–5s.** Extreme close-up: Nadia's phone vibrates on the table. Message overlay: “Jangan pulang bersamanya malam ini.” Her hand freezes above it.

**5–13s.** Nadia reads it twice. Her breathing changes.  
NADIA (under breath): “Siapa, sih?”

**13–22s.** Arka enters carrying two takeaway drinks.  
ARKA: “Kamu kenapa? Dari tadi aku panggil.”  
NADIA: “Nggak. Cuma ada pesan aneh.”  
ARKA: “Spam kali. Sini, kita pulang.”

**22–34s.** Arka puts his phone on the table. A notification from a contact saved as “R” flashes. He turns the phone face down.  
NADIA: “R siapa?”  
ARKA: “Teman kantor.”

**34–47s.** Nadia discreetly photographs the notification reflection. Arka watches her.  
ARKA: “Kamu nggak percaya sama aku?”  
NADIA: “Aku cuma nanya.”

**47–56s.** Arka heads to the door. Nadia's phone vibrates. Overlay: “Jangan biarkan dia membawamu ke parkiran.”

**56–60s.** Nadia looks at Arka, then toward the dark corridor. New overlay: “Sekarang lihat ke belakangmu.” Cut to black. END CARD: “BERSAMBUNG”.

## EP02 — DIA BERBOHONG (60 sec)
**0–4s.** Phone insert, morning. Message: “Dia berbohong soal tadi malam.”  
NADIA (V.O.): “Aku seharusnya mengabaikan pesan itu.”

**4–14s.** Nadia checks the photo she took and searches her chat with Arka.  
NADIA: “Katanya semalam kamu langsung pulang.”

**14–25s.** Arka appears on video call, avoiding eye contact.  
ARKA: “Iya. Kenapa?”  
NADIA: “Terus kenapa lokasimu ada di tempat lain?”  
ARKA: “Kamu ngecek lokasi aku?”

**25–38s.** Nadia opens an anonymous location screenshot showing a café after Arka claimed he was home.  
ARKA: “Itu bisa aja data lama.”  
NADIA: “Bisa. Tapi kamu belum jawab.”

**38–50s.** Call ends. A blurred image arrives with overlay: “Kamu belum melihat semuanya.”

**50–60s.** Nadia enlarges the image. In the café window reflection, a woman stands behind Arka.  
NADIA (whispers): “Siapa perempuan itu?” Cut to black.

## EP03 — FOTO BESOK (60 sec)
**0–5s.** Nadia receives a photo. Message: “Besok, pukul 19.14. Jangan datang sendirian.”

**5–17s.** The image shows a café entrance after rain and a figure from behind wearing a silver ring identical to Nadia's.  
NADIA (V.O.): “Tempat itu belum pernah kudatangi.”

**17–29s.** Nadia compares her ring with the photo.  
NADIA: “Ini editan?”

**29–42s.** She checks the location on a map and writes the address on paper.

**42–53s.** A voice note arrives. SENDER (V.O., Nadia's voice): “Kalau kamu datang, jangan percaya orang pertama yang menyapamu.”

**53–60s.** Nadia replays it, frozen.  
SENDER: “Nadia… dengarkan aku.” Cut to black.

## EP04 — ORANG KETIGA (60 sec)
**0–5s.** Nadia enters the café from the photo. A woman at the corner table looks up.  
RANI: “Nadia?”

**5–16s.**  
NADIA: “Kita kenal?”  
RANI: “Belum. Tapi aku tahu kenapa kamu datang.”

**16–29s.** Rani slides a printed screenshot across the table.  
NADIA: “Kamu yang kirim pesan?”  
RANI: “Bukan. Aku juga menerimanya.”

**29–42s.** Nadia sees a photo of Arka with Rani.  
NADIA: “Jadi kalian…”  
RANI: “Dia bilang kalian sudah selesai. Aku baru tahu itu bohong.”

**42–53s.** Rani points to the sender details.  
RANI: “Nomor ini selalu berubah. Tapi satu hal sama: dia tahu kejadian sebelum terjadi.”

**53–60s.** Rani leans in.  
RANI: “Dan pesan terakhirnya menyebut namamu.”  
Nadia's phone lights up: “Jangan percaya Rani sepenuhnya.” Cut to black.

## EP05 — IDENTITAS (60 sec)
**0–6s.** Nadia replays the voice note.  
NADIA: “Itu suaraku.”

**6–18s.** She compares it with an old voice memo. The cadence and breath are identical.  
RANI (PHONE): “Aku sudah bilang. Pengirimnya tahu terlalu banyak.”

**18–31s.** Nadia opens message details. A synced contact label appears: “NADIA”.  
NADIA: “Kenapa namaku ada di sini?”

**31–43s.** A new audio file downloads.  
SENDER (V.O.): “Aku nggak punya banyak waktu.”

**43–54s.**  
NADIA: “Kamu siapa?”  
SENDER: “Kamu. Tapi bukan yang sekarang.”

**54–60s.** A date exactly seven days earlier appears. Message: “Buka rekaman yang kamu sembunyikan.” Cut to black.

## EP06 — TUJUH HARI SEBELUMNYA (65 sec)
**0–6s.** Nadia searches an old cloud folder and finds a video dated seven days earlier.

**6–20s.** The recording shows a woman matching Nadia's clothes and ring, but only her shoulder and hand.  
NADIA-2 (V.O.): “Kalau kamu menonton ini, berarti aku gagal.”

**20–34s.**  
NADIA-2: “Arka bukan satu-satunya yang menyembunyikan sesuatu. Jangan ikuti semua pesanku.”

**34–47s.** Present Nadia pauses the video. In its background, a framed photo is broken—something that has not happened yet.

**47–58s.** The recording resumes.  
NADIA-2: “Ada satu pesan yang harus kamu abaikan. Pesan dari aku yang berikutnya.”

**58–65s.** Nadia's phone lights up: “Jangan percaya aku.” Cut to black.

## EP07 — JANGAN PERCAYA AKU (60 sec)
**0–5s.** Incoming call from Nadia's own number.

**5–17s.** Nadia answers.  
SENDER: “Jangan keluar malam ini.”  
NADIA: “Kamu yang menyuruhku datang selama ini.”

**17–29s.**  
SENDER: “Aku mencoba memperbaikinya.”  
NADIA: “Atau kamu sedang mengarahkanku ke sesuatu?”

**29–42s.** A knock at the door. Arka speaks from outside.  
ARKA: “Nad, buka. Kita harus bicara.”  
The phone voice whispers: “Jangan buka.”

**42–53s.** Nadia does not open. She activates the hallway camera and records the door. A shadow moves away.

**53–60s.** Message: “Bagus. Sekarang jangan lakukan kesalahan yang sama.” Nadia types: “Kali ini aku yang menentukan.” Cut to black.

## EP08 — PESAN TERAKHIR (70 sec)
**0–7s.** Nadia places two phones on a table and starts a screen recording.  
NADIA (V.O.): “Selama ini aku mengikuti petunjuk. Sekarang aku akan menguji siapa yang mengirimnya.”

**7–20s.** She sends Arka a false meeting location and sends the same location to the anonymous account.

**20–34s.** At the café exterior, Arka arrives first. He scans the street, then calls someone.

**34–47s.**  
ARKA (into phone): “Aku sudah bilang, jangan kirim pesan lagi ke dia.”  
SENDER (through speaker, Nadia's voice): “Kamu terlambat.”

**47–58s.** Nadia steps into view.  
NADIA: “Kalian berdua kenal siapa aku. Sekarang jelaskan.”  
Arka turns, stunned. Nadia's phone vibrates.

**58–66s.** Message: “Kamu berhasil mengubah satu hal.” The timestamp reads tomorrow.

**66–70s.** Final message: “Tapi besok, jangan percaya siapa pun.” Nadia looks into camera. Cut to black. END CARD: “SEASON 1 SELESAI”.

- Social platforms: publish natively through authorized account owner; no posting is claimed by this document.
- Apify: optional public trend research only after explicit approval of scope and any spend. No paid scraping is authorized here.

## Genspark production sequence
1. Create one Media Studio project for the season.
2. Generate and approve character references and the three location references.
3. Add cast, wardrobe, props, palette, camera rules and geography to Story Bible.
4. Create a separate board/node for each shot; attach correct references.
5. Test difficult shots first: Nadia face, Arka/Nadia two-shot, phone insert, ring, café two-shot.
6. Review the live credit estimate before every Run. Cost depends on model, resolution, duration, number of outputs and complexity.
7. Generate only approved shots; log model, settings, estimated/actual credits, output ID and selection reason.
8. Regenerate only failed shots, not whole episodes.
9. Export selected clips to editor. Add all legible UI and text in post.
10. Assemble, subtitle, sound mix, QC and archive.

**Credit discipline:** storyboard and lock references before video generation. Start with Episodes 1–2 as a pilot. Set a spending ceiling only after inspecting the current account balance and live estimate. No paid top-up or paid scraping without explicit approval.

## Post-production specs
- MP4 H.264, 1080×1920, 9:16, consistent 24/25 fps, AAC audio.
- Dialogue intelligible above music; no clipping.
- Indonesian subtitles, maximum two lines, high contrast, safe margins.

- Thumbnail: one readable face and 2–5 words.
- Keep clean masters without platform watermark; upload separately and natively.

## QC checklist
**Story:** first two seconds establish a question/threat; one dramatic turn per episode; understandable without sound; ending creates a specific unanswered question; no continuity contradiction.

**Visual:** stable faces, wardrobe, ring, phones and locations; sender face concealed until Episode 6; coherent eye-lines; no impossible reflections or duplicated props.

**Technical:** correct vertical frame and safe margins; accurate subtitles; legible post-composited UI; balanced audio; no watermark/artifacts; plays correctly on phone.

**Rights and safety:** licensed music/SFX/fonts; no unlicensed celebrity or private-person likeness; no private phone numbers/accounts/addresses; no misleading claim that AI scenes are documentary evidence.

## Release strategy
- Publish Episode 1 as pilot.
- If quality and audience response justify continuing, publish Episodes 2–4 at a steady daily cadence, then 5–8.
- Use short teasers only if they do not spoil the mystery.
- After finale, prepare a 2–4 minute compilation for YouTube/Facebook where appropriate.
- Do not release all eight at once during the first test; preserve ability to observe return behavior.
- Test posting times against account analytics rather than assuming a universal best time.

Metrics differ across platforms; do not compare raw views as equivalent. Record platform, publish time, episode, duration, hook/caption version, reach/views, watch time, completion indicators, shares, saves, comments, follows, comment themes, generation credits and editing hours. Review at 24h and 72h.

Interpretation:
- High views / low completion: hook may work, pacing or payoff may be weak.
- Lower views / high completion: packaging or distribution may need testing.
- High comments / low shares: inspect whether discussion is meaningful or confusion-driven.
- High shares and follows: stronger evidence of resonance, not proof of long-term demand.
