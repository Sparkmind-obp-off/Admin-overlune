# AVORLUNE Media Studio Service Portfolio & Capability Map

**Status:** Proposed service portfolio / documentation baseline  
**Owner:** AVORLUNE  
**Provider reference:** Genspark Media Studio  
**Last reviewed:** 2026-11-03

## 1. Purpose

This document defines how AVORLUNE packages the capabilities available in Genspark Media Studio into customer-facing creative production services. AVORLUNE is acquiring the capability as a service workflow, not acquiring Genspark, its software, underlying models, infrastructure, or intellectual property.

Genspark is an external production provider. AVORLUNE owns the customer relationship, creative brief, creative direction, production planning, quality control, delivery coordination, and portfolio presentation, subject to the provider's terms and the rights applicable to every input and output.

## 2. Positioning

**AVORLUNE — AI Creative Production**

AI-assisted image, video, audio, motion and 3D production, directed by a human creative process and reviewed through human quality control.

The portfolio is organized by customer outcome rather than by the internal Genspark feature names. Genspark Media Studio is the initial production engine; other providers or manual tools may be substituted where appropriate.

## 3. Service families

### A. Film & Storytelling

1. **AI Short Film** — a standalone narrative film with a setup, turn and ending. Initial validated scope: 30 seconds to 3 minutes per the Genspark short-film recipe.
2. **AI Short Drama / Episodic Drama** — serialized narrative chapters with recurring characters, locations, story bible, screenplay and continuity control.
3. **AI Brand Film** — cinematic brand storytelling, lifestyle film and campaign film; Genspark's documented recipe starts at 60 seconds and supports dialogue/subtitles.
4. **AI Trailer / Teaser** — promotional cutdowns for films, launches, products or campaigns. Motion can support exact title cards, graphic overlays and trailer-style compositions.
5. **Interactive Film** — branching narrative with decision points and multiple endings; export/publishing requires the documented HTML build and GenCode workflow.

### B. Advertising & Commercial Video

1. **AI Video Ads / Social Ads** — vertical, square or landscape paid/organic creative, with hook, benefit, proof, offer and CTA.
2. **Product Ad / TVC** — product-led commercial. Documented Genspark recipe: 15–60 seconds, product reference connected across shots, storyboard before rendering, composer, voice-over and music.
3. **Product Showcase** — product hero, macro, lifestyle and specification visuals plus a short showcase video.
4. **Brand Commercial** — brand or product campaign with recurring cast, dialogue, music, subtitles and a brand ending.
5. **UGC-style Ad Creative** — creator-led or testimonial-style creative. Real-person likeness, endorsements and testimonial claims require explicit authorization and factual substantiation.
6. **Campaign Variations** — alternate hooks, edits, aspect ratios, language versions and platform-specific cutdowns from an approved master.

### C. Social & Content Production

1. **Short-form Social Videos** — TikTok, Reels, Facebook Reels and YouTube Shorts.
2. **Content Repurposing** — turn long-form recordings, interviews or talks into several captioned vertical clips.
3. **Explainer Videos** — product, service, onboarding, education and process explainers.
4. **Narrated Report / Presentation Videos** — transform approved documents, slides or design pages into narrated motion videos.
5. **Music Videos** — storyboard by song section and generate shots edited to the beat. Music rights must be cleared.
6. **Podcast / Interview Clips** — identify strong moments, cut, crop, caption and package clips.

### D. Image, Design & Brand Assets

1. **AI Campaign Visuals** — key art, campaign imagery, social visuals and supporting image sets.
2. **Product Listing Visuals** — hero, macro, lifestyle, specification infographic and banners.
3. **Brand Kit / Visual Identity Support** — style direction, logo concepts, style guide, packaging and launch poster. Final identity and trademark clearance remain human/client responsibilities.
4. **Poster & Key Art** — editable poster or one-sheet; typography and legal billing details require proofing.
5. **Character & Mascot Design** — turnaround, expression sheet, poses and reference assets for recurring use.
6. **Image Editing & Layered Deliverables** — retouch, crop, layer separation, editable PSD export where supported.

### E. Audio & Voice

1. **Voice-over / Narration** — approved script narration in selected language and voice.
2. **Dialogue & Dubbing** — character dialogue and language versions using approved voice references.
3. **Music & Sound Design** — music and sound-effect generation or selection, subject to license/usage review.
4. **Audio-led Content** — podcast-style or narrated audio deliverables.
5. **Audio Post-production** — dialogue, effects and music lanes mixed into the final cut.

### F. Motion, 3D & Interactive

1. **Motion Graphics / Animated Explainers** — exact titles, captions, lower thirds, charts, numbers, HUDs, transitions and logo end cards.
2. **3D Previsualization** — block spatial relationships, camera movement and action before expensive final video generation.
3. **3D World / Product Visualization** — generated or imported 3D environments and models, within supported formats and account availability.
4. **Interactive Pages / Animated Pages** — HTML motion or interactive page experiences.
5. **Interactive Film Packaging** — branching film build for supported web, desktop and mobile publishing routes through GenCode.

## 5. Service packaging

Use three delivery levels. Final names and pricing require owner approval.

### Starter — Single Asset
- One image, short clip, product visual or small creative asset.
- One approved brief and one output specification.
- Limited revision allowance defined in the quote.
- No promise of source project unless explicitly included.

### Campaign — Multi-asset Package
- A coordinated set of ads, product visuals, social clips or campaign variations.
- Shared creative direction and visual references.
- Master asset plus agreed platform cutdowns.
- Deliverable manifest and usage notes.

### Film / Series — Narrative Production
- Development, story bible, screenplay, storyboard, shot plan, production, editing and final delivery.
- Chapter/episode breakdown and continuity records.
- Approval gates at story, bible, storyboard, rough cut and final cut.
- Production schedule and credit budget confirmed before rendering.

Do not publish fixed price points until pilot costs, labor, revisions, storage, licenses and margin have been measured.

1. Inquiry and use-case classification.
2. Creative brief and source-material intake.
3. Rights, likeness, music and claim review.
4. Scope, deliverables, format, timeline and quote.
5. Deposit and production authorization under the existing AVORLUNE service-payment policy.
6. Creative direction and preproduction approval.
7. Generation and review.
8. Edit, sound, subtitles and finishing.
9. Client review and agreed revisions.
10. Final delivery, archive and optional distribution package.

## 7. Deliverable formats

Agree each format in the signed brief. Typical outputs may include:
- Video: MP4/H.264, vertical 9:16, square 1:1, landscape 16:9; resolution and frame rate confirmed per project.
- Images: PNG/JPEG; PSD only where layered export is supported and included.
- Audio: WAV/MP3 or provider-supported export.
- Documents: PDF/Markdown for scripts, story bible, storyboard notes and production manifest.
- Editable project: Premiere or CapCut project only when export is available and explicitly included.
- Interactive: HTML/build package through supported GenCode workflow.

## 8. Boundaries and truth-in-marketing

- Do not claim AVORLUNE owns Genspark or its models.
- Do not promise every feature is available on every account, plan, workspace or region. Verify access in the active account before quoting.
- Do not promise a fixed number of generations for a fixed credit amount without checking the live configuration estimate.
- Do not promise perfect identity, lip-sync, text rendering, product fidelity or continuity; use review and reshoot allowances.
- Do not advertise "unlimited" generation.
- Do not guarantee platform reach, ad approval, conversion or sales.
- Do not use a real person's likeness, voice, private footage, logo or copyrighted music without the necessary permission.
- Do not fabricate testimonials, product results, before/after claims or regulated claims.
- Distinguish AI-generated imagery from documentary footage where context requires transparency.

## 9. Pilot validation before public launch

Validate at least:
1. One 30–60 second product ad.
2. One 30–60 second brand film.
3. One 60–90 second short drama with recurring character references.
4. One social repurposing batch from a long-form source.
5. One motion graphics explainer.
6. One sample image/brand kit deliverable.
7. One interactive film proof-of-concept only if the account has the required export/publishing access.

Record actual credits, generation count, accepted-shot ratio, manual editing time, render time, file size, revisions and final quality. Only validated services may be marked production-ready.
