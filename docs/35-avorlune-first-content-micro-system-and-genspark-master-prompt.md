## 1. Mission

Produce the first publishable AVORLUNE TikTok video now. Do not wait for a full content automation platform.

This micro-system is deliberately small:
1. one clear audience problem;
2. one finished script;
3. one voice-over direction;
4. one visual storyboard;
5. one Genspark generation prompt;
6. one publication package;
7. one human quality-control gate.

The deliverable is a rendered video draft ready for human review and, after approval, TikTok upload. A prompt or generated draft is not itself a published post.

## 2. First-video decision

**Working title:** Kamu Bukan Kekurangan Aplikasi

**Opening hook:** “Kamu bukan kekurangan aplikasi. Kamu mungkin terlalu banyak menggunakan aplikasi untuk mengurus satu pekerjaan.”

**Core idea:** Adding more digital tools does not automatically make work easier. When information, tasks, files, and communication are scattered across too many places, the person doing the work may spend more effort switching and searching than progressing. The first improvement is to understand the workflow and simplify it before adding another tool.

**Audience:** Indonesian creators, freelancers, small-business owners, and digitally busy individuals.

**Primary pillar:** P01 — The Overload Problem: Digital Noise & Friction.  
**Secondary pillar:** P02 — Clarity, Focus & Digital Organization.

**Audience awareness:** Problem-aware; the viewer recognizes the feeling but may not have named it.

**Desired response:** Recognition, saves, comments, and profile curiosity. No hard sell.

**Editorial stance:** Helpful and observant, not scolding. Do not imply that using many apps is always bad. The problem is unnecessary fragmentation and unclear workflow—not the number of apps by itself.

## 3. Creative specifications

- Aspect ratio: 9:16 vertical.
- Target duration: 25–32 seconds; do not pad to reach a number.
- Resolution: highest practical vertical export available in Genspark.
- Visual style: premium, minimal, modern, cinematic editorial technology.
- Palette: deep charcoal / near-black, warm white, restrained electric blue or muted violet accents. Avoid excessive neon, clutter, and generic sci-fi interfaces.
- Editing: deliberate cuts, subtle motion, clean transitions, readable typography.
- Narration: natural Indonesian, calm, confident, conversational; not robotic, exaggerated, or salesy.
- Captions: accurate Indonesian subtitles, synchronized to narration, high contrast, inside TikTok-safe margins.
- Music: optional low-volume royalty-cleared or platform-licensed instrumental. Voice must remain clear. If licensing is uncertain, omit music.
- No visible third-party logos, fake app interfaces, fabricated dashboards, or invented AVORLUNE product screens.

## 4. Final voice-over script

Read naturally, with short pauses. Do not add unsupported claims.

“Pernah merasa pekerjaan makin banyak, padahal aplikasi yang kamu pakai juga makin banyak?

Satu aplikasi untuk catatan. Satu untuk tugas. Satu untuk file. Belum lagi chat dan berbagai notifikasi.

Akhirnya, kamu bukan cuma mengerjakan pekerjaan. Kamu juga sibuk mencari, berpindah, dan mengingat semuanya ada di mana.

Mungkin solusinya bukan menambah aplikasi baru.

Mulai dari satu hal: rapikan alur kerjamu. Lihat apa yang benar-benar dibutuhkan, lalu sederhanakan prosesnya.

Karena teknologi seharusnya membantu kita lebih jelas, bukan menambah kerumitan.

Ini Overload. Mari ubah kompleksitas menjadi kejelasan.

AVORLUNE.”

**Pronunciation:** AVORLUNE may be pronounced “A-vor-lun” or according to the owner's chosen brand pronunciation. Use one consistent pronunciation across future videos.

## 5. Scene-by-scene storyboard

| Time | Visual | Voice-over / on-screen text |
|---|---|---|
| 0–3s | Close-up of a person at a desk, laptop and phone showing many generic notification shapes; controlled visual clutter. | VO: “Pernah merasa pekerjaan makin banyak…” Text: **MAKIN BANYAK APLIKASI?** |
| 3–7s | Fast but clean sequence: notes, task list, files, chat windows represented by generic unlabeled UI cards. | VO: “…padahal aplikasi yang kamu pakai juga makin banyak?” Text: **CATATAN. TUGAS. FILE. CHAT.** |
| 7–13s | Cursor switches between generic windows; a file search; person pauses, overwhelmed. | VO: “Akhirnya, kamu bukan cuma mengerjakan pekerjaan…” Text: **WAKTU HABIS UNTUK BERPINDAH.** |
| 13–18s | Visual clutter gradually clears; screen transitions to a simple, organized workflow on paper or abstract cards. | VO: “Mungkin solusinya bukan menambah aplikasi baru.” Text: **JANGAN TERBURU-BURU TAMBAH TOOLS.** |
| 18–25s | Three clear steps appear: map workflow → identify essentials → simplify. | VO: “Mulai dari satu hal: rapikan alur kerjamu…” Text: **PAHAMI. PILIH. SEDERHANAKAN.** |
| 25–30s | Calm dark background, subtle light movement, elegant AVORLUNE wordmark if a verified brand asset is supplied. | VO: “Teknologi seharusnya membantu kita lebih jelas…” Text: **FROM COMPLEXITY TO CLARITY.** |
| 30–32s | End card: OVERLOAD / by AVORLUNE. | VO: “Ini Overload. … AVORLUNE.” Text: **OVERLOAD — BY AVORLUNE** |

Timing is a guide. Let the voice-over and edit determine the final cut. If Genspark cannot reliably render legible text, generate clean visuals without text and add all titles/subtitles in a separate editor.

## 6. Genspark.ai master generation prompt

Copy the following prompt into the video-generation workflow available in Genspark. If the tool requests separate fields, place the narration in the script/voice field and scene directions in the visual/storyboard field.

---

**PROMPT START**

You are a professional short-form video creative director, Indonesian voice-over producer, motion designer, and social media editor working for AVORLUNE.

Create one polished, publishable-first-draft TikTok video for the editorial concept OVERLOAD by AVORLUNE.

### Creative objective
Make Indonesian viewers recognize that using more digital applications does not necessarily make work simpler. Show how fragmented tools and scattered information can create friction, and offer one useful first step: understand and simplify the workflow before adding more tools.

### Audience
Creators, freelancers, small-business owners, and digitally busy people in Indonesia.

### Format
- Vertical 9:16.
- Target 25–32 seconds.
- Designed for mobile viewing.
- Strong visual hook in the first 1–2 seconds.
- Natural Bahasa Indonesia narration.
- Accurate synchronized subtitles.
- Premium editorial technology aesthetic.
- Clear voice intelligibility and controlled pacing.

### Voice-over
Use this exact script. Do not rewrite, translate, add statistics, or invent claims:

“Pernah merasa pekerjaan makin banyak, padahal aplikasi yang kamu pakai juga makin banyak?

Satu aplikasi untuk catatan. Satu untuk tugas. Satu untuk file. Belum lagi chat dan berbagai notifikasi.

Akhirnya, kamu bukan cuma mengerjakan pekerjaan. Kamu juga sibuk mencari, berpindah, dan mengingat semuanya ada di mana.

Mungkin solusinya bukan menambah aplikasi baru.

Mulai dari satu hal: rapikan alur kerjamu. Lihat apa yang benar-benar dibutuhkan, lalu sederhanakan prosesnya.

Karena teknologi seharusnya membantu kita lebih jelas, bukan menambah kerumitan.

Ini Overload. Mari ubah kompleksitas menjadi kejelasan.

AVORLUNE.”

Voice direction: natural Indonesian speaker, calm, intelligent, warm, clear articulation, conversational confidence. Avoid robotic delivery, exaggerated advertising energy, or dramatic shouting. Maintain a slightly reflective opening and a reassuring ending.

### Visual direction
Use premium, minimalist, cinematic technology editorial visuals:
- deep charcoal and near-black backgrounds;
- warm white typography;
- restrained electric blue or muted violet accents;
- realistic desk and device imagery;
- generic abstract app cards with no recognizable third-party logos;
- controlled visual clutter that gradually becomes organized;
- elegant transitions and subtle camera movement.

Avoid:
- generic AI robot imagery;
- fake software interfaces presented as real products;
- unreadable generated text;
- random code rain;
- excessive neon;
- stock-looking handshake imagery;
- fabricated AVORLUNE platform screens;
- visual elements that distract from the narration.

### Scene order
1. Immediate relatable desk/device overload visual.
2. Generic representations of notes, tasks, files, and chat.
3. Visual switching/searching friction.
4. Visual pause and reduction of clutter.
5. Simple three-step workflow: Pahami → Pilih → Sederhanakan.
6. Premium brand end card: OVERLOAD — BY AVORLUNE.

### Text and subtitles
Use concise Indonesian on-screen emphasis:
- MAKIN BANYAK APLIKASI?
- CATATAN. TUGAS. FILE. CHAT.
- WAKTU HABIS UNTUK BERPINDAH.
- JANGAN TERBURU-BURU TAMBAH TOOLS.
- PAHAMI. PILIH. SEDERHANAKAN.
- FROM COMPLEXITY TO CLARITY.
- OVERLOAD — BY AVORLUNE

Keep subtitles accurate to the actual narration. Do not let generated text obscure the subject or fall outside mobile safe areas. If text rendering is unreliable, leave clean space for subtitles and end-card typography to be added in post-production.

### Sound
Prioritize clean voice-over. Use only music that is confirmed licensed for the intended use, or generate without music. Do not include recognizable copyrighted songs or sound-alike tracks.

### Output
Generate one complete vertical video draft, not a montage of unrelated options. If the workflow supports it, also provide a clean visual version without burned-in subtitles so the editor can add accurate captions.

Do not claim the video is published. Do not add fake views, comments, endorsements, product claims, or platform UI. Return the rendered video and any available editable project assets.

**PROMPT END**

## 8. Production checklist

Before generation:
- [ ] Confirm the Genspark workflow supports the desired vertical video output.
- [ ] Use an approved AVORLUNE logo/wordmark asset if available; otherwise use plain text.
- [ ] Do not upload private customer assets or sensitive information.

After generation:
- [ ] Watch the entire video.
- [ ] Check that narration is complete and naturally pronounced.
- [ ] Check every subtitle against the approved script.
- [ ] Check 9:16 framing and mobile-safe margins.
- [ ] Check for malformed text, strange hands/faces, visual glitches, and fake interfaces.
- [ ] Confirm music and visual asset rights.
- [ ] Confirm there are no unsupported product claims.
- [ ] Ensure the ending brand text is correct.
- [ ] Export the final approved file in the best practical quality.

**Approval gate:** The owner must approve the final rendered video and caption before publishing. Generation does not authorize automatic TikTok posting.

## 9. First-post measurement

Record the actual results after publication; do not fabricate or estimate them.

Capture at a consistent review interval where TikTok analytics make the data available:
- views;
- average watch time;
- completion rate;
- retention curve;
- shares;
- saves;
- comments;
- profile visits;
- follows.

Review the comments for audience language and recurring problems. The next video should respond to observed questions or test a distinct angle—not simply repeat the first video's hook.

## 11. Reusable micro-system instruction

For every future Overload / AVORLUNE TikTok request:

1. Select one primary audience and one recognizable problem.
2. Select one primary content pillar from document 34.
3. State one central message and one distinct angle.
4. Write a hook that is relevant and truthful.
5. Produce a concise Indonesian narration.
6. Translate narration into a scene-by-scene visual storyboard.
7. Generate a Genspark-ready prompt with style, aspect ratio, voice, scenes, subtitles, and sound direction.
8. Create the TikTok cover, caption, CTA, and relevant hashtags.
9. Apply evidence, brand, copyright, and product-claim checks.
10. Require human review before publication.
11. Record actual analytics and use them to improve the next content item.

**Do not overproduce.** One clear, finished, reviewed video is more valuable than a large batch of unreviewed AI drafts.
