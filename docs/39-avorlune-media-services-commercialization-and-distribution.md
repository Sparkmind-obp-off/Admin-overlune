# AVORLUNE Media Services — Commercialization, Pricing & Distribution Framework

**Status:** Commercial framework / pilot required before public rate card  
**Last reviewed:** 2026-11-03

## 1. Commercial objective

Turn the production capabilities documented in Media Studio into understandable, purchasable AVORLUNE services. Sell finished creative outcomes, not raw Genspark credits or access to Genspark.

Customer pays AVORLUNE for a scoped production service. AVORLUNE uses Genspark and other approved tools as production infrastructure. Do not represent the service as an official Genspark reseller, partner or white-label product unless a written agreement authorizes that status.

## 3. Quote structure

Each quote must be built from:

1. Creative development and direction.
2. Preproduction (script, storyboard, Bible, references).
3. Media generation (estimated provider credits and model mix).
4. Human editing and finishing.
5. Voice, music, sound and subtitles.
6. Revisions and reshoots allowance.
7. Storage, transfer and archive.
8. Project management and QC.
9. Rights, licensing and third-party costs.
10. Margin and contingency.

Internal cost model:

TOTAL COST = provider generation spend + human production hours + post-production + licensed assets + storage/transfer + project overhead + contingency.

SELLING PRICE = total cost / (1 - target gross margin).

Do not expose provider credit balances as a customer-facing currency. Quote deliverables, scope, duration, formats, revisions and delivery dates.

## 4. Pricing policy

Do not publish a fixed rate card until pilots provide real cost data. Start with a custom quote and a clearly defined scope.

For every service, specify:
- Runtime and number of deliverables.
- Aspect ratios and resolution.
- Number of scenes/shots or creative variants.
- Whether script/storyboard/Story Bible is included.
- Voice-over, dialogue, music and subtitle inclusion.
- Source/editable project inclusion.
- Number of revision rounds.
- Customer-provided materials and rights.
- Delivery date and rush fee if applicable.
- Usage/licensing limitations.
- Archive and retention period.

Out-of-scope changes, new concepts, additional languages, extra formats, new characters, major script changes and additional reshoots require a change order.

## 5. Pilot cost study

Run a controlled pilot for:
- One 15–30 second product ad.
- One 30–60 second brand commercial.
- One 60–90 second short drama with one recurring character.
- One 3–5 minute episodic proof-of-concept only after the shorter test.
- One podcast repurposing batch.
- One motion graphics explainer.

For each pilot capture:
- Number of planned and generated shots.
- Model, duration, resolution and estimated/actual credits per shot.
- Accepted first-pass ratio.
- Regeneration count and reason.
- Manual editing hours.
- Audio and subtitle effort.
- Export and archive effort.
- Total turnaround time.
- Customer quality score and revision count.

Use pilot data to establish service-level tiers and a safe credit reserve. Do not infer that 1,000 or 3,000 credits always equals a specific film length.

## 6. Sales and fulfillment flow

1. Public catalog describes outcomes and sample deliverables.
2. Lead submits a brief and references.
3. AVORLUNE checks fit, rights and feasibility.
4. AVORLUNE sends scope, quote, timeline and payment terms.
5. Production starts only after required deposit and approval.
6. Customer approves creative direction, script and storyboard.
7. Production and QC.
8. Review link / watermarked preview if appropriate.
9. Collect final balance under existing service policy.
10. Deliver approved master and included assets.
11. Offer cutdowns, campaign variants or next episode.

Existing service payment policy remains 50% deposit before production and 50% balance before final delivery, unless the owner formally changes it. This is a service fulfillment rule, not an Admin platform payment or subscription product.

## 7. Distribution packages

### Organic social package
- Platform-specific aspect ratios and safe areas.
- Captions, cover, description and CTA.
- Episode sequencing and cliffhanger planning for serial drama.
- Delivery-ready exports; posting is a separate authorized action.

### Paid advertising package
- Multiple hook/creative variants.
- Product/offer proof and CTA.
- Platform-specific ad format and copy.
- Claims, disclaimers and product accuracy review.
- Customer/ad-account approval before publishing.
- No guarantee of ad approval, impressions, conversions or sales.

### Film / series package
- Full master and chapter-level exports.
- Trailer/teaser and selected cutdowns.
- Episode title cards, thumbnails, captions and descriptions.
- Continuity bible and approved asset archive if included.
- Distribution plan for TikTok, Facebook, Instagram and YouTube.
- Platform upload/publishing only when separately authorized and operationally available.

## 8. Rights and claims checklist

Before production:
- Customer owns or licenses supplied assets.
- People appearing or whose voice/likeness is used have provided appropriate permission.
- Music, fonts, stock, logos and trademarks are cleared for intended channels and paid usage.
- Product specifications and claims are verified by customer.
- No fabricated testimonials, reviews, endorsements or performance claims.
- No misleading synthetic depictions of real events or people.
- Platform disclosure and advertising policies are checked for the destination.

The contract should clarify rights in customer inputs, generated outputs, project files, provider terms, third-party model terms and AVORLUNE portfolio usage. Do not promise exclusive copyright or unrestricted rights without legal review and provider-term confirmation.

## 9. Launch sequence

### Phase 0 — Internal validation
- Learn the active Genspark account's actual feature access.
- Test a product ad, short film, audio and motion workflow.
- Verify collaboration, export and archive.
- Measure costs and human hours.

### Phase 1 — Limited service launch
Start with the most repeatable:
- Product / social video ads.
- Short-form social content.
- Short film / brand film pilot.
- Product and campaign visuals.

### Phase 2 — Expanded production
After validation:
- Episodic drama.
- Podcast repurposing at batch scale.
- Voice/dubbing and multilingual versions.
- Motion graphics and narrated explainers.

### Phase 3 — Advanced
Only after technical proof:
- 3D-heavy sequences.
- Interactive films and GenCode publishing.
- Larger series and multi-account productions.

## 10. Public claims policy

Allowed after capability verification:
- "AI-assisted creative production."
- "Short film, episodic drama and video ad production."
- "Creative direction and human quality control."
- "Multi-format delivery for social and campaign channels."

Avoid:
- "Powered exclusively by proprietary AVORLUNE AI."
- "Unlimited AI video."
- "Guaranteed cinematic realism."
- "Guaranteed viral / conversion results."
- "Official Genspark partner" without authorization.
- "All features available on every plan."
