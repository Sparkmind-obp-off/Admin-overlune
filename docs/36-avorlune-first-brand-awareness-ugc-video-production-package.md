## 1. Production intent

Create a first brand-awareness video that introduces AVORLUNE through a relatable observation about AI-assisted video creation.

The video must feel like a real creator sharing an insight, not a corporate advertisement. Its job is to establish a memorable point of view:

> A good AI-assisted video is not only about generating attractive visuals. It needs a clear idea, story, creative direction, and human quality control.

AVORLUNE must be introduced accurately as **AI Creative Production**, with creative direction and human quality control. Do not invent platform features, customer results, production capabilities, guarantees, or service packages that have not been confirmed.

This is a brand introduction—not a hard-sell offer and not a product demo.

## 2. Creative brief

### Audience
- Indonesian content creators experimenting with AI.
- Small-business owners who need marketing content.
- Marketers and social media operators.
- People interested in AI-assisted image and video production.

### Audience problem
People can generate attractive AI visuals, but may still struggle to decide what the video should communicate, how scenes connect, and what makes the result relevant to its audience.

### Core message
**Visual yang bagus saja belum cukup. Video membutuhkan cerita dan arah.**

### Brand role
AVORLUNE introduces an approach to AI creative production where creative direction and human quality control matter alongside AI-assisted production.

### Desired audience response
1. Recognize the problem.
2. Understand the difference between visual generation and purposeful creative production.
3. Remember the AVORLUNE name and brand descriptor.
4. Become curious about future AVORLUNE content.

### Tone and performance
- Conversational, warm, intelligent, and confident.
- Natural Indonesian delivery.
- A creator speaking to a friend or sharing a useful observation.
- No exaggerated acting, aggressive selling, corporate narration, or unsupported superiority claims.

## 3. Hook alternatives

Choose one hook for the first production. The recommended version is Hook A.

### Hook A — Relatable problem (recommended)
“Pernah nggak, bikin video pakai AI, hasilnya bagus… tapi sebenarnya nggak tahu mau menyampaikan apa?”

Why: It describes a recognizable experience and creates an immediate reason to keep watching.

### Hook B — Contrarian curiosity
“Menurutku, masalah terbesar bikin video AI bukan di tools-nya.”

Why: It creates curiosity and opens a discussion about creative direction.

### Hook C — Direct insight
“Video yang bagus itu bukan cuma harus terlihat keren. Harus punya cerita.”

Why: It communicates the brand's point of view quickly and simply.

## 6. Global Genspark visual direction

Apply these instructions to all scene generations:

- Vertical 9:16 composition, designed for phone screens.
- Realistic creator-generated UGC footage, not a glossy corporate commercial.
- Natural window daylight and believable indoor lighting.
- Modern but lived-in workspace, not an unrealistic luxury office.
- Handheld smartphone camera with subtle natural movement.
- Authentic human expressions, gestures, and timing.
- Understated, clean, contemporary visual treatment.
- Keep the same creator, clothing, and workspace across scenes.
- Avoid excessive depth-of-field, artificial skin, over-smoothing, dramatic lens flares, and generic futuristic AI imagery.
- Do not generate subtitles, logos, brand names, interface text, watermarks, or fake product screens. Add typography and the verified logo during editing.

If Genspark supports image/character references, create and approve one creator reference image first, then reuse it for each scene. If it does not, repeat the same creator description and visual continuity instructions in every prompt.

## 7. Genspark prompts by scene

Generate each scene as a separate clip. Do not rely on a single all-in-one generation to preserve identity, timing, and editability. Use the actual available Genspark workflow and do not assume a feature exists if it is not present in the owner's account.

### Scene 01 — Creator talking to camera (4 seconds)

```text
Create a 4-second vertical 9:16 authentic UGC video.

A young Indonesian content creator sits at a simple modern home workspace, speaking directly to a smartphone camera. The creator looks naturally into the lens with a slightly curious expression, as if sharing a personal observation with a friend.

Use realistic handheld smartphone footage, natural window daylight, medium close-up framing, subtle camera movement, authentic facial expressions and natural lip movement.

The mood should feel spontaneous, relatable and trustworthy, not like a corporate commercial.

Keep the same creator appearance and clothing for future scenes.

No subtitles, no text, no logos, no watermark. No exaggerated acting. No cinematic slow motion.
```

### Scene 02 — The visual overload / problem (5 seconds)

```text
Create a 5-second vertical 9:16 realistic UGC B-roll video.

Show the same creator from the previous scene sitting at their workspace, reviewing several different AI-generated visual concepts on a laptop. The creator scrolls through the visuals, pauses, and looks slightly uncertain about how to connect them into one coherent story.

Use an over-the-shoulder smartphone camera angle, natural daylight, realistic laptop reflections, subtle handheld motion and believable everyday movements.

The footage should look like genuine behind-the-scenes creator content.

No readable text, no fake interface labels, no logos, no watermark.
```

### Scene 03 — Ideas and storyboard (7 seconds)

```text
Create a 7-second vertical 9:16 authentic behind-the-scenes video.

Show the same creator's hands planning a short video at a desk. The creator writes ideas in a notebook, sketches simple storyboard frames, arranges sticky notes and reviews visual references on a laptop.

Use a top-down handheld smartphone perspective with natural daylight and realistic hand movements. Include close-up details of pen strokes, notebook pages and paper arrangements.

The atmosphere should feel practical, thoughtful and achievable, like a real independent creator preparing a production.

No generated readable words, no logos, no watermark, no overly polished commercial styling.
```

### Scene 04 — Production and quality control (8 seconds)

```text
Create an 8-second vertical 9:16 realistic UGC behind-the-scenes video.

Show the same creator reviewing video footage and creative references on a laptop. The creator carefully compares a visual reference with a generated video clip, pauses to inspect the result, then makes a small adjustment.

Use a natural over-the-shoulder shot followed by a close-up of the creator's hand using the trackpad. Realistic screen lighting, natural room light, subtle handheld smartphone movement.

Communicate thoughtful creative review and human quality control rather than automatic one-click production.

Do not generate readable interface text, logos, captions or watermarks.
```

### Scene 05 — Creator close and brand reveal (10 seconds)

```text
Create a 10-second vertical 9:16 brand closing sequence.

Begin with the same Indonesian creator looking directly into the smartphone camera with a calm, confident and friendly expression. The creator finishes speaking naturally.

Transition to a minimal premium editorial background with subtle movement, clean composition, refined lighting and understated modern visual identity.

Leave generous empty space in the center for a brand logo and typography to be added during editing.

The brand mood is modern, thoughtful, sophisticated and creative. Avoid flashy effects, excessive gradients, neon colors and generic futuristic AI imagery.

Do not invent or generate a logo, brand name, readable text or watermark.
```

## 8. Voice-over and audio direction

### Voice
- Natural Indonesian speaker.
- Warm, clear, conversational confidence.
- Slightly reflective opening; reassuring and assured ending.
- Avoid robotic cadence, exaggerated advertising energy, and forced enthusiasm.
- Keep the exact approved script; no invented statistics or extra promises.

### Recording
- Record in a quiet room.
- Keep microphone distance consistent.
- Record one complete take, then individual lines as backups.
- Remove distracting noise without making the voice sound artificial.
- If generated voice is used, review pronunciation of “AVORLUNE” and all Indonesian words.

### Music and sound effects
- Optional low-volume instrumental background.
- Use only music confirmed licensed for the intended channel and commercial use, or omit it.
- Keep dialogue clearly above music.
- Use subtle transitions only; avoid excessive whooshes and impacts.

## 9. Editing instructions

### Edit order
1. Open immediately on the hook; no logo intro.
2. Cut to the laptop visual problem.
3. Move into notebook/storyboard details.
4. Show creative review and quality control.
5. Return to creator for the brand introduction.
6. Finish with a clean end card.

### Pacing
- Remove pauses that do not add meaning.
- Keep the hook immediate and understandable.
- Use natural jump cuts and short B-roll inserts.
- Avoid changing shots so frequently that the message becomes hard to follow.
- Allow the brand name and descriptor enough time to register.

### Subtitles and text overlays
- Use accurate Bahasa Indonesia subtitles synchronized with the final audio.
- Maximum two lines at a time.
- Highlight only key phrases: **AI**, **CERITA**, **CREATIVE DIRECTION**, **HUMAN QC**.
- Use a clean sans-serif font and strong contrast.
- Keep all text within TikTok-safe margins.
- Add text in post-production rather than relying on AI-rendered typography.

### Brand treatment
- Use only the approved AVORLUNE logo/wordmark asset.
- If no approved logo file is available, use plain editable text “AVORLUNE”.
- Brand descriptor: **AI Creative Production**.
- Use a restrained neutral palette and the established brand identity assets if available.
- Do not invent a new logo, symbol, or brand color system in the video-generation step.

### Export
- 1080 × 1920 preferred.
- Vertical 9:16.
- MP4 / H.264 where available.
- 30 fps preferred, or preserve the source frame rate if the editor requires it.
- AAC audio where available.
- Export one clean master and retain source clips / project files if available.
